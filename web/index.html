Model accuracy

Checked nightly: the model forecasts 30 days from three past dates and is compared with what actually happened. WAPE = Σ|actual − forecast| / Σ actual (lower is better). Range coverage should be close to 80%.

How this forecast is made

TimesFM 2.5 (Google) — a 200M-parameter pretrained time-series foundation model. It reads up to 2,048 days of registration history and forecasts each future day with a 10th–90th percentile range, without any training on CAM-IE data. Monthly and yearly totals are the sum of the daily forecasts, so all three views agree.

Automatic backup: Holt-Winters ETS. If TimesFM can’t load, errors, or returns an implausible forecast (empty, near zero, or far above recent levels), the nightly job switches to ETS automatically so the graph is never blank. The badge in the header shows which model produced the current forecast.
